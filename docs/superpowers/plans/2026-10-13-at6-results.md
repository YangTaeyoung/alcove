macOS 27.0.1, Swift 6.4, Node 26.10.0, Python 3.10.4, QEMU 11.1.1 with
`qemu-patches/0001` to `0003`) against the VM `Windows 11` (Windows 11 Pro

VM bundle were copied from the machine of the earlier AT6 runs (Apple M3 Pro);

The runs below used `at6-e2e.py` at 0346367. The script changed after them
(see [Changed after these runs](#changed-after-these-runs)); those changes are

## Defects found and fixed

| Defect | Cause | Fix | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| SIGTERM to `run-script --snapshot` during the restore left the VM paused with its tools disconnected (`vm.status` → `state: paused`, `tools: false`); `vm.resume` brought it back at the snapshot | the server cancels a client's tasks when its connection closes. `VMController.restoreSnapshot` had stopped the guest; the cancelled task left the wait for the `snapshot-load` job with `CancellationError`, and the `cont` in its error path threw at once because `QMPClient.execute` checks cancellation first, so `cont` never went out. QEMU's `snapshot_load_job_bh` resumes only a VM that was running when the job began (`build/qemu-src/migration/savevm.c:3612-3631`), and the controller had stopped it before | once the guest is touched, the stop, load and resume run in a task of their own that the caller's cancellation does not reach; a caller cancelled before that point gets `CancellationError` without a stop (2910684) | `VMControllerFakeEngineTests.testRestoreWhoseCallerIsCancelledDuringTheLoadFinishesAndResumesTheGuest` (fake engine mode `slow-load`, load of 1 s); failed before the fix with `the controller state is paused after the cancelled restore` and `the engine reports Status(running: false, state: "paused")`; passes after it. The SIGTERM run above passes |

Test-side changes, not product defects:

- `at6-e2e.py` cleanup waits up to 120 s until the VM runs with its tools and
  no lease (0346367). With the fix above, a run cancelled during the restore
  keeps its lease on the server until the restore has ended (a few seconds
  after `run-script` exited), and the cleanup's `vm.clipboard.set` and
  `vm.snapshot.delete` were refused with `VM 'Windows 11' is leased by
  script:scenario.json …`.
- `at2-e2e.py` expects the 13th `vm.doctor` row, `proxy`, which AT7 added
  (425925a).

## Changed after these runs

To be re-run on the guest: `python3 scripts/test/at6-e2e.py`, once with the
snapshot path (at least 18 GiB free) and once without.

- Checks 3, 4 and 6 expect the typed message exactly once in the
  transcript, so a `--snapshot` that restores nothing fails check 3.
- Check 9 runs a script that writes a start mark, waits for a release file
  that a guest job writes 15 s after it started, presses Send and writes an
  end mark. A run without a signal shows the click and the end mark. Each
  signal is sent once the start mark exists; the lease must be gone within
  5 s of the exit, and 10 s after the release file appeared the fixture log
  holds no click and the end mark is absent.
- Check 12 signals once a `vm.pull_file` step (step 4) has written its host
  file, so the interrupted step is step 5.
- Check 13 fails on a Kernel-Power 41 entry instead of printing the count.
- The cleanup deletes `at6-base` also after a signal during
  `vm.snapshot.create` when `vm.snapshot.list` lists it, and keeps waiting
  while the VM is starting or stopping.
- A snapshot restore that is running makes a second one wait, and a stop or
  suspend during its load is no longer overwritten by the restore's resume
  (`VMController.restoreSnapshot`); a cancelled caller's agent reconnect no
  longer retries without a pause (`AgentLink`).

## Not verified

- The app as one of the two hosts in check 10 and check 13: the app may not
  be launched in these sessions.
- The stop-failure exit status of `run-script --in-process`:
  `VMController.shutdown(force: false)` falls back to QMP `quit` and then to a
  kill after its 60 s grace and throws only when the controller is not active
  (`VMController.swift`), so `HeadlessRegistry.stopAll` gets no failure from a
  live VM.
- `alcove_script_run` with a failing script (the content of the failure image
  through MCP); check 6 covers the passing run.
- A signal to `run-script --in-process` during a `--snapshot` restore (the
  socket-mode case is covered; both run the same `VMController.restoreSnapshot`).
- `vm.snapshot.create` or `vm.snapshot.delete` whose caller is cancelled: QEMU's
  own job resumes the guest after a save, and those jobs are left undismissed
  in QEMU's job list; no run exercised it.
