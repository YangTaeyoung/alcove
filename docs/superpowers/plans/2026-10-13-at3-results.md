## Measurements

`alcovectl` is the debug build the scripts start (`.build-ctl/debug`) unless a
column says release (`swift build -c release`). Each latency is the mean over
the calls of one loop; a range gives the means of the loops that were run.

### Screenshots: frame path and `screendump`

The same loop of 20 `vm.screenshot` calls per format and scale, two or three
loops each, once with the server as it is and once with
`ALCOVE_FRAME_SOURCE=screendump` in its environment. Frame 1280x800 showing
the desktop.

| Format, scale | Image | Frame path, debug | `screendump`, debug | Frame path, release | `screendump`, release |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PNG, 1 | 2.41 MB | 120 ms to 129 ms | 261 ms to 264 ms | 119 ms to 126 ms | 228 ms to 260 ms |
| PNG, 0.5 | 580 KB | 34 ms to 39 ms | 174 ms to 176 ms | 25 ms to 27 ms | 141 ms to 145 ms |
| JPEG, 1 | 660 KB | 13 ms to 17 ms | 149 ms to 157 ms | 13 ms | 125 ms to 188 ms |
| JPEG, 0.5 | 153 KB | 15 ms to 16 ms | 160 ms to 165 ms | 7 ms to 10 ms | 117 ms to 125 ms |

Reading the frame costs about 110 ms to 150 ms through `screendump` and a few
milliseconds through the shared framebuffer; what remains on the frame path is
the encoding (a full-size PNG takes more than 100 ms of the 120 ms). Check 1 of
the script asserts a mean of at most 60 ms for JPEG at scale 0.5: it measured
14 ms to 16 ms in every run and 158 ms, with `FAIL 1`, against the server
started with `ALCOVE_FRAME_SOURCE=screendump`.

### Text recognition

`vm.find_text`, 20 calls each, Notepad with the two lines of the script open.

| Area | Lines read | Debug | Release |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole frame, default languages | 31 | 122 ms | 128 ms |
| Whole frame, `languages: ["en-US"]` | 29 | 65 ms | 63 ms |
| Region 640x200 | 8 | 48 ms | 50 ms |
| Region 320x64 (enlarged twofold) | 2 | 42 ms | 43 ms |

The first recognition with a recognizer after a server start took 16.4 s to
20.7 s for the default languages (four starts) and 15.5 s to 43.7 s for
`["en-US"]` (seven starts). One further start, of a release binary that had
run three minutes before, read at once (0.2 s). Recognitions after the first
took the times of the table.

### Input with an observation

Ten `vm.mouse.click` per level, alternating between two points in Notepad's
text, `settleMs` 300 and `settleTimeoutMs` 2000 (the defaults); every
observation reported `settled: true`.

| `observe` | Debug, mean (range) | Release, mean (range) | Returned image |
|---|---|---|---|
| `none` | 67 ms (64 to 70) | 66 ms (63 to 71) | none |
| `summary` | 649 ms (585 to 784) | 418 ms (390 to 468) | none |
| `diff` | 671 ms (578 to 791) | 400 ms (374 to 424) | 96x512 PNG, 11.7 KB; the reply is 15.8 KB of JSON |
| `image` | 726 ms (610 to 838) | 508 ms (472 to 568) | 1280x800 PNG, 1.22 MB; the reply is 1.63 MB of JSON |

`vm.type` of two characters with `diff` returned a 288x512 PNG of 29.1 KB in
749 ms (debug) and 478 ms (release). With `ALCOVE_FRAME_SOURCE=screendump` the
release build needed 638 ms, 609 ms and 651 ms for `summary`, `diff` and
`image`. A click on a screen that keeps changing, with `settleTimeoutMs: 800`,
returned `settled: false` after 0.94 s to 1.06 s (check 6 asserts at most 2 s,
which a click that waits for the default `settleTimeoutMs` of 2000 ms cannot
meet).

### Batch

Ten steps (five `vm.mouse.move`, five `vm.key.press` of `shift`), ten
repetitions.

| | Debug, mean (range) | Release, mean (range) |
|---|---|---|
| One `vm.batch` | 259 ms (238 to 276) | 249 ms (234 to 281) |
| The ten ops one by one on one connection | 274 ms (238 to 289) | 261 ms (238 to 294) |
| The ten ops written at once (pipelined), one run | 251 ms | 267 ms |

A batch is not faster on the control socket: about 200 ms of the time are the
pauses of the five key presses themselves. What it adds is that the VM's input
gate is held once for all steps and that a caller needs one round trip.

### Cost of waiting

CPU time of `alcovectl` (`ps -o time`) over a `vm.wait_for` of 30 s that ends
unmatched, desktop on the screen.

| Conditions | Debug | Release |
|---|---|---|
| `screen_idle` and `text_visible` | 19.5 s (65 % of one core) | 6.3 s (21 %) |
| `screen_idle` | 15.6 s (52 %) | 0.7 s (2.4 %) |
| `text_visible` | 8.2 s (27 %) | 6.3 s (21 %) |
| no request for 30 s | 0.01 s | 0.01 s |

The frame comparison of the debug build takes about 130 ms per sample; the
release build reads and compares a sample in about 6 ms. A recognition costs about 0.15 s
of CPU, 43 times in 30 s. With `ALCOVE_FRAME_SOURCE=screendump` the 30 s
`screen_idle` wait cost 1.9 s in `alcovectl` (release) and 10.1 s in the
engine process, against 0.9 s to 2.8 s in the engine on the frame path.

## The measured answers

**Which recognizer reads what.** A lossless frame of the guest with 31 known
lines in a maximized Notepad (15 with Latin letters, 16 with Hangul; 11-point
Consolas and Malgun Gothic at 100 % scale, about 15 pixels high) was
recognized outside the server with the server's settings, as the whole frame,
its left half and 292 bands (1280x400, 1280x200, 1280x100, 640x100, 640x60 and
400x40 at steps of 7 to 23 pixels). A line counts when a recognized line
contains it, ignoring spaces and case.

| Languages | Enlargement | Latin lines read | Hangul lines read |
|---|---|---|---|
| `["ko-KR", "en-US"]` (default) | none | 352 of 575 (61 %) | 565 of 590 (96 %) |
| `["ko-KR", "en-US"]` | twofold | 322 of 575 (56 %) | 567 of 590 (96 %) |
| `["ko-KR", "en-US"]` | threefold | 331 of 575 (58 %) | 535 of 590 (91 %) |
| `["en-US"]` | none | 516 of 575 (90 %) | 0 of 590 |
| `["en-US"]` | twofold | 502 of 575 (87 %) | 0 of 590 |

On the whole frame the default languages read 9 of the 15 Latin lines and
returned, among others, `File not found: report-2026.xISx`, `Username on
password is incorrect`, `C: lUserslalcovelDocumentslnotes.txt`, `Enron
0x80070005: access denied` and `Youn trial ends in 7 days`; they read 15 of
the 16 Hangul lines. `["en-US"]` read 14 of the 15 Latin lines. In the guest
the default languages read the script's line `Alcove OCR check` as `Alcove OR
check` or `Alcove OFR check` in 12 of 12 readings over two launches, and five
runs of the script in a row failed a 60 s wait for it; later runs read it
correctly in 6 of 7. `["en-US", "ko-KR"]` read no Hangul either. Enlarging
the frame before recognition changes which lines are misread, not how many: a
build that enlarged every frame twofold read `batch works` where the build
without enlargement read `batch wonks!`, and read `한글 인직 확인` in a
1280x98 region where the other read `한글 인식 확인`.

**What a batch validates.** `vm.batch` refuses a batch before running it for
a step that is no object, an op that is not allowed, another VM's `name` and a
`sleep` without a valid `ms`. A wrong parameter of an allowed op is found when
its step runs: `{"op": "vm.key.press", "keys": "nosuchkey"}` as third step ran
the two steps before it and answered `failedStep: 2`; a `vm.type` without
`text` as second step answered `failedStep: 1`.

**Notepad.** `vm.exec.start` with `argv: ["notepad.exe", <file>]` and `shell:
"none"` returns the pid of a launcher process that stays alive; the window
belongs to a child process of it. `vm.process.kill` with that pid and `tree:
true` answered `killed: 1` and ended both. Notepad came up with the tab it had
saved before and the file in a second tab; while it ran, its `LocalState`
folder gained one file for the new tab and the saved files kept their
modification times. Started this way the window is not in the foreground until
it is clicked: the first click changed a 928x768 or 960x768 box, a later click
in the text a 64x448 box.

A change that enlarged every area up to 1600 pixels per side twofold before
recognition was committed after it corrected `batch wonks!` and four other
lines in two frames, and taken back after the comparison over 31 lines and 294

Hangul in a region the script reads. The rule is again that areas narrower
than 400 pixels are enlarged.

## Checks adapted to the guest

- **Recognition languages.** The plan's check 2 waits for `Alcove OCR check`
  and its check 4 searches `batch works` with the default languages. With
  them the Korean recognizer reads both lines wrongly on some boots. The
  script passes `languages: ["en-US"]` for the lines in Latin letters and the
  default for the Korean line, and prints in the note of check 2 how the
  default languages read the English line in that run.
- **Killing Notepad.** The plan kills by `processName`. The script kills the
  pid `vm.exec.start` returned, with `tree`, and waits with `process_exited`
  by name and by pid. It refuses to start when a Notepad process runs already.
- **Notepad's saved tabs.** Before the first launch the files of
  `LocalState` are copied to the host with `vm.pull_file` (the path is
  printed). Every later launch and the cleanup first end the run's Notepad
  processes, then delete the files that are not in the copy and write the
  copied ones back with `vm.push_file`, comparing digests.
- **Latency bounds.** Check 1 asserts a mean of at most 60 ms (measured 14 ms
  to 16 ms; 158 ms on the `screendump` path). Check 6 asserts at most 2 s
  (measured 0.94 s to 1.06 s).

## What differed from the plan

- `observe` takes `none`, `summary`, `diff` and `image`; there is no level
  `full`.
- Check 2 also covers the `detail` of the match, the UTF-8 round trip through
  `vm.exec`, an empty query, a region and the two language refusals; check 4
  the refused batch and the observation of a failed one; check 5
  `process_started`, a closed port and two conditions in one wait; check 6
  `job_output_matches`, `screen_idle` and a settled click afterwards; check 7
  `observe: "none"` and a search without a match.
- `docs/control-protocol.md`: the frame source and `ALCOVE_FRAME_SOURCE`, what
  a batch checks before it runs, the `languages` refusals, which recognizer
  reads which script, rectangles inside the searched area, the first
  recognition after a server start, the recognition that outlasts a timeout
  and the port check sentences. The MCP descriptions of `alcove_find_text`
  and `alcove_wait_for` name the `languages` to pass.
- `README.md` lists the three tools of this work already and was not changed.

## Open

- With the default languages a line of small Latin text is read correctly
  about six times in ten. A caller has to know to pass `["en-US"]`, and a
  screen with both scripts has no setting that reads both well. Reading the
  frame with both recognizers and keeping, per line, the Korean reading where
  it holds Hangul and the English one elsewhere was not built or measured.
- The first recognition after a server start takes 16 s to 44 s on this
  machine. `vm.find_text` waits for it (the MCP server gives the call 60 s);
  `vm.wait_for` no longer does. The server does not load the recognizers
  before the first request.
- Guest frames larger than 1280x800 were not recognized in the guest. Outside
  the server, a 1280x800 frame of the guest placed on a plain larger canvas
  lost lines as the canvas grew: 7 of 9 chosen lines were read at 1280x800, 8
  at 1600x1000, 6 at 1920x1080, 2 at 2560x1600 and none at 3840x2160; enlarged
  twofold, 9, 9, 6, 0 and 0.
- A recognition in a `vm.wait_for` blocks the other conditions of that wait
  until it ends or is given up.
- The debug build of `alcovectl` spends half a core on a `screen_idle` wait;
  the release build 2.4 %. The scripts start the debug build.
- `vm.wait_for` conditions were not run against a Linux guest, and the MCP
  tools `alcove_wait_for` and `alcove_batch` were not driven in the guest
  (their unit tests cover them).
- A background `SystemSettings` process with a hidden `Settings` window
  appeared in the guest during the first run of the script; nothing of it
  shows on the screen or the taskbar, and the script neither started nor ended
  it.
- `README.md` still states 53 tools; the server lists 74 since the `ui`,
  `evidence` and `http` toolsets of the later work were added.
